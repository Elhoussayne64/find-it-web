Privacy Policy — Find It

Developer: ElAr Games
Application: Find It (com.elargames.findit)
Last updated: November 20, 2026

ElAr Games ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information Find It collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect Directly

Find It does not require an account, login, name, email address, or any personal profile. Your game progress, unlocked rooms, coins, settings, and privacy preferences are stored locally on your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Find It displays optional rewarded video ads and occasional interstitial ads through Google AdMob (unless you purchase "Remove Ads"). Depending on your age group and consent choices, Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, approximate location, and ad interaction or diagnostic data to serve, measure, and prevent fraud in ads.

  • Non-personalized by default: Ads are requested as non-personalized unless players aged 13+ explicitly opt in.
  • EEA / UK users: Players aged 13+ are shown Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form where required by law.
  • You can review or change your privacy choices at any time from Settings → Privacy inside the game.

Learn more about how Google uses data: Google Privacy & Terms.

3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional in-app purchases (such as "Remove Ads") are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details or billing address. We only receive confirmation from Google Play that a product has been purchased so we can unlock it in the game.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families)

On first launch, Find It displays a neutral age screen. Players who select Under 13 are automatically placed in kid-safe mode:

  • No personalized or interest-based advertising is requested.
  • All ad requests are tagged for child-directed treatment with a maximum ad content rating of G.
  • No analytics or usage data is sent off the device.

5. Analytics

Find It does not send personal data off your device. If anonymous gameplay statistics are ever enabled by an adult/teen opt-in in a future update, they will only contain non-personal gameplay events (such as level started or completed) and will never be transmitted in kid-safe mode.

6. Data Deletion

Because your game data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Find It. To reset or limit advertising identifiers, use your Android device's Settings → Google → Ads menu.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. If material changes are made, the game will prompt you to review your choices again.
